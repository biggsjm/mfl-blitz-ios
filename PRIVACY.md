# Privacy policy

Effective September 13, 2026

League-data implementation reviewed against **0.6.0 (33–35)**; the optional DEBUG-only NFL test section below was added September 9, 2026. Those builds added protected Trading Block/Calendar snapshots and drafts, optional local deadline notifications, selected-event Apple Calendar handoff and a matchup Live Activity. Installation evidence is in [current status](docs/current-status.md). Sign-in and league actions still go directly to MFL. The optional private background-scoring service is described below; its sandbox Apple push setup was activated September 13, 2026.

MFL Blitz is an independent, open-source iOS client for MyFantasyLeague. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking, crash-reporting, or proprietary chat service.

## Data the app handles

- Your MFL username, password, league ID, and season are sent directly from your device to MyFantasyLeague over HTTPS when you sign in.
- Your password is used only for that login request. MFL Blitz does not persist it.
- The MFL session value is stored in this device’s Keychain, accessible while unlocked and not synchronized to iCloud Keychain or migrated to another device. It is used to reconnect after app termination; your password is never saved.
- League information requested from MFL can include rosters, player ownership/status and optional biography, fantasy season schedules, scores, standings, owner names, pending waiver bids, trade offers and comments, tradable assets, transaction history, and message-board content. Most response caches are memory-only; the private disk exceptions are described next. Owner names are displayed only as provided by MFL; the app does not look up owners elsewhere.
- Last-loaded scores/projections, lineup, standings, team identity, own-team season roster and Board list summaries may be stored in one size-bounded display file. Each section is usable for cached display only while younger than seven days; expired-file retention is explained below. It does not retain full Board posts or pending bids/trade offers. A separate daily metadata file retains MFL's league export, which may include owner names/contact fields and bid balances supplied by MFL. It is reused only after fresh membership verification; balance display and submissions require stricter freshness.
- Both private cache files use iOS complete file protection, are excluded from backup and bound to the exact saved session/season/league/franchise. Cache identifiers contain a one-way session hash, not the raw cookie. These are display/read optimizations, not permission or offline submission records. iOS may evict them. The [cache contract](docs/performance-startup.md) specifies size, expiry and account-isolation checks.
- The public NFL player directory has a separate season-specific copy in the app’s disposable Caches folder for up to 24 hours and is reused after reopening. It contains public player data, not credentials, owner details, private league data, or response headers. iOS may remove it; the next successful download replaces it.
- Targeted player biographies have a separate 24-hour memory cache; they are not added to the persisted public directory. Schedule, roster and player browsing is scoped to the current account/league, and late responses from an old session cannot populate the new account.
- Lineup drafts, queued waiver edits, board and trade drafts, and unconfirmed-action markers are stored in the same device-only Keychain, scoped to season, league, and franchise. Trade drafts and markers include offer terms, optional comments, expiration, and identifying information needed to verify the action. Markers prevent an interrupted send from being automatically repeated after relaunch.
- Team icons and logos use the HTTPS artwork URLs supplied by your league. Images may be hosted by MFL or an external website. Artwork requests use a separate session that sends no account cookie or credentials, accepts no image-host cookies, and has no disk cache. Small, static thumbnails are cached only in memory, including the signed-in team logo displayed in the My Team tab.

No MFL login credentials, drafts or private league actions are sent to an MFL Blitz server. When private background scoring is connected, the limited matchup data described below is sent to your configured server. Apple and MyFantasyLeague may process network or platform data under their own policies. Artwork hosts also receive your IP address and the requested image URL under their own policies; they do not receive your MFL sign-in or other league API responses.

The player-tools increment also reads public injury/schedule/bye feeds, league-scored player history and points allowed, the owner's watchlist and action permissions. These responses are memory-only. Watchlist stars send incremental add/remove requests directly to MFL; roster moves send only the reviewed player IDs. Watchlist markers retain player ID, desired state and time; roster markers retain scope, requested move, expected membership and time. Both are device-only Keychain data used for readback/duplicate prevention, not analytics.

## Sharing and tracking

Trading Block and Calendar read the signed-in league's listings, asset ownership, owner/team names, event titles and dates. The last-loaded snapshots, block draft/publication marker and reminder preferences are stored in device-only Keychain items scoped to season, league and franchise. Each snapshot is bounded to 1 MB and used for display for at most seven days; older saved content may remain until replacement or disconnect. Ordinary fresh reads reuse five-minute block and fifteen-minute calendar caches. Cached content never authorizes a publication or a new reminder. Block publication sends only the owner's reviewed assets/needs directly to MFL, which makes them visible to the league.

Deadline reminders are off until you choose an event/category and allow notifications. iOS receives a generic event-kind label, deadline/fire time and minimal league/franchise/event identifiers for opening the right screen; no credentials, bids, trade comments or private custom event titles are placed in notification content. These reminders may appear on your Lock Screen according to your system settings. They use local scheduled notifications, not a server or APNs token. Turning a reminder off removes the app's corresponding alert; Disconnect removes app-owned pending/delivered deadline notifications.

Add to Apple Calendar hands one selected event title/date to Apple's system editor. You choose Save or Cancel. Blitz does not request permission to read your calendars, does not inspect your existing events, and does not maintain a subscription. Saved events remain in your chosen calendar under Apple's/account provider's settings; later MFL changes do not update that one-time copy, and Disconnect does not remove it.

The optional matchup Live Activity gives iOS the two team names/abbreviations, scores, live team estimates, small logo thumbnails/colors, active-player count, week and minimal matchup/scope identifiers. Its latest observed change can include a starter's name and fantasy-point delta, or a team/batch summary, with check timestamps. This can be visible on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. The September 13 logo update uses the existing cookie-free artwork transport, a maximum 900-byte thumbnail per team and no additional shared disk cache. No cookie, credentials, player injury detail or trading/waiver data is included. A device preference, last activity identifier and a dismissed-matchup identifier are stored in app preferences so the same dismissed activity is not immediately recreated. Disable it in Blitz Settings or iOS; Disconnect ends it and clears dismissal tracking. Ordinary builds update while Blitz is foregrounded and mark older scores stale. Push-enabled builds can connect to the optional service below; the installed build’s status states whether Apple setup is required.

### Optional private background scoring

A push-enabled build sends the current matchup’s season/week, league and franchise IDs, team abbreviations, participating starter IDs/names and pregame projections, scores, live estimates, small logos/colors, and ActivityKit update token to your configured private server over Tailscale HTTPS. Your server reads the configured public MFL scoring feed without your login, checks approximately every 60 seconds, and sends score content to Apple for Lock Screen delivery. Apple receives the activity token, app topic and displayed content; MFL receives the server’s address and requested league/week. Tailscale supplies your login identity to the private service to enforce its owner allowlist. The app sends a device-only random subscription secret, whose hash is stored on the server; it is unrelated to your MFL login.

Subscription tokens, reduced score baselines and display content live in an owner-only SQLite database for at most 7h50m from initial registration. Removed data is securely deleted from SQLite; raw MFL responses and request logs are not retained. Revocation tombstones keep only activity IDs and expiry for another 7h50m to reject late requests. Disable, disconnect or observed dismissal requests deletion; when the service is unreachable, a device-only Keychain queue retries on the next foreground connection or end, and entries expire after eight hours. A suspended app may not observe dismissal immediately; server token invalidation and expiry bound retention. A stopped server cannot prune until restarted. A bounded local history retains the latest 30 lifecycle messages and timestamps on the phone; the server retains 64 lifecycle reasons/timestamps. These diagnostics contain no activity IDs, team/player names, tokens or credentials and remain until replaced or the app/service data is removed. Server backups, if independently configured by the owner, are outside the app’s control.

The APNs signing key stays in a separate owner-only server file and is never included in the app or activity. No MFL password, session cookie, waiver/trade information or drafts leave the device for this feature. The private service and sandbox Apple account/profile/key setup are active. Physical-device verification is tracked separately in the current status. See the [background-scoring runbook](docs/background-scoring.md).

MFL Blitz does not sell or share personal information and does not track you across apps or websites. The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.

## Private current-season NFL scoring (builds 55–56)

When a configured build opens matchup/player scoring, it sends the selected NFL season/week and public NFL team codes used to prioritize the viewed games to the owner's private Hephaestus server over Tailscale HTTPS. It sends no MFL login, franchise IDs, MFL player IDs, fantasy scores, owner information or player-search queries. Matching game players to the MFL roster happens on the phone using name, NFL team and position, or reviewed MFL/provider ID translations supplied by the server. The server's versioned translation table contains public player IDs/names, season, NFL team/position and review evidence; it contains no league ownership or user information. The provider receives only the server's existing API-Sports credential, NFL endpoint parameters and network address.

One server-side worker/cache serves all readers under a persistent request budget. Viewing demand expires five minutes after the last read. The app pauses its reader when backgrounded, retains up to three weeks in memory and displays original source receipts; the server retains game and roster responses until replaced or deliberately removed. No new analytics, request-access logging or public endpoint is introduced. The private service address is a build setting; the provider credential never ships in the app. This current-season feed is separate from the manual historical test below and from MFL fantasy-point calculation. See [live NFL scoring](docs/live-nfl-scoring.md) for refresh and retention behavior.

Build 56 limits repeated completed-game correction reads to the first week after kickoff, followed by one final requested archive read. Later viewing reuses that saved box and its original receipt; known matching identity metadata remains available for final games. Fast player-stat polling only runs during active game states. Upcoming-game discovery and slower final-correction checks also share the same server budget and viewing-demand limits.

## Game-day features (build 57)

Optional lineup notifications start off. Enabling an option requests Apple's notification permission and sends the app push token, environment, public roster IDs/names/NFL teams, season/league/franchise/week, required starter count and selected alert options to the same private Hephaestus service. MFL credentials and draft starters are never sent. The server checks saved MFL lineups and injury/schedule reports before kickoff and sends notification text and a scoped Lineup destination through APNs. Registrations expire after eight days and are renewed when the current lineup is opened. Turning off options or disconnecting queues server removal; an offline removal is retained privately in this device's Keychain for retry. Until removal succeeds, the prior subscription can still send alerts. Delivery deduplication and revocation markers expire after eight days. Notification content may appear on the Lock Screen according to iOS settings.

The matchup timeline retains observed public MFL score changes for 21 days, at most 2,000 per matchup. On the phone it uses a protected, backup-excluded Application Support file, limited to 12 matchups for the selected workspace. Disconnect removes this local history. The existing background worker separately retains at most 64 matchups in its private SQLite database; shared history remains until the 21-day limit or deliberate server removal. It records only while subscribed matchup checks run, and a timeline read does not start another upstream poller. Timelines do not store credentials, drafts, chat messages or individual NFL plays.

Live Activity subscriptions now also include public starter NFL teams/positions. The private worker can read its existing NFL cache for next-kickoff and player-stat context without receiving the provider key. This activity demand expires along with the existing subscription. D/ST enrichment sends requested public NFL defense team codes to that same NFL cache and remains inside its shared request ceiling. League-specific points breakdowns are calculated on the phone from cached public MFL scoring rules and available stats; official MFL points remain unchanged.

## Optional private NFL historical test (development builds only)

My Team → Settings → NFL stats test is an explicit, manual historical-data evaluation. It sends only the selected NFL season, game ID and (when reviewing identity) API-NFL player ID to the owner's private Hephaestus server over Tailscale HTTPS. It sends no MFL credentials, league/team identifiers, ownership, player searches, drafts or fantasy scores. The server uses a separate API-Sports key in an owner-only file to retrieve public NFL game/player statistics and selected profiles. API-Sports receives that credential, the requested NFL parameters and the server's network address. This historical feature enables no tracking SDK or polling and is compiled out of release builds. The separate current-season feed is described above.

Optional reviewed player matches reuse the app’s cached MFL player catalog locally. Confirmation stores the two public IDs, profile/name/position and game-team evidence, historical and MFL catalog seasons, private service origin, dates and your verification note in separate developer app preferences. They are not transmitted to Hephaestus, API-Sports or MFL, and are not used by live player cards. **NFL stats test → Reviewed player matches → Remove match** deletes a saved pair; the player’s review has the same action. Forget server and MFL Disconnect do not delete these independent reviewed pairs. They are not synced by the app, but ordinary device backup may include app preferences. Synthetic Preview matches stay in memory only.

The phone stores the private server address in app preferences and keeps test responses in memory. **Forget server** removes that preference and displayed test data; Disconnect does not remove this developer setting because it is independent of an MFL league. The server caches successful historical responses for 24 hours, may return clearly marked saved data under seven days old on failure, and preserves request-budget/cooldown counters across restarts. Inactive records remain until replacement or deliberate server cleanup. It logs no client requests or identities. This is a shared private NFL test cache, not league storage. Tailscale processes connection/device data under its own settings; HTTPS certificates publish the machine hostname in the public certificate registry, not the API key or stats requests.

Missing values are not interpreted as zeroes or converted into fantasy points. The synthetic Champion Hall preview never connects to the service. See the [test-service contract](docs/nfl-stats-service.md) for limits and remaining validation.

## Retention and deletion

Disconnecting a live team clears its saved session, local drafts, unconfirmed-action markers, in-memory league data and private cache files. Session expiry preserves drafts so you can recover them by signing in to the same franchise; cached startup display is rejected without its matching saved session. Closing the app retains the protected session/drafts and the cache files described above, while other private response caches disappear from memory. Expired cache entries are not used and are replaced on a successful download; unused expired files may remain until replacement, disconnect, iOS eviction or uninstall. Uninstalling removes app cache files. Data already submitted to MFL remains on MyFantasyLeague. Resolve any unconfirmed post, trade, watchlist or roster action on MFL before disconnecting, since disconnecting removes the local duplicate-prevention markers too.

Meaningful trade edits are autosaved privately for interruption recovery. Cancel restores the draft that existed before the editor opened, with a discard confirmation for changes; opening an empty composer alone does not retain a draft. Discard draft deletes that local draft, not offers already sent to MFL. Uninstalling may not remove Keychain items; use Disconnect first to remove the team's protected data.

Meaningful Board edits are autosaved for interruption recovery. Close asks whether to retain the draft, discard it, or keep editing. Saved new threads and replies are accessible from Board → Drafts; a thread can also Resume reply. Discard deletes only that draft, not any MFL post or unresolved-send marker. Empty composers do not retain a draft. Save/discard failures keep the composer open for retry.

## Connected-league actions

Trading Block adds an explicit owner-reviewed publication workflow. A fresh membership/permission/asset check and full-list baseline comparison precede the only send; exact readback confirms it. A timeout leaves a protected pending marker and Check status, never an automatic resend. Block drafts are saved when you choose Save draft or before a publication attempt; Discard deletes the draft, not a published listing. Build 35 adds confirmed whole-list removal: both asset and needs fields are explicitly emptied, and a successful fresh export must confirm absence or empty terms. An empty removal draft is recoverable like other edits. Cash listings remain on MFL. Preview block edits, reminder choices and calendar screens use synthetic memory data without publishing, scheduling real alerts or saving calendar events.

This private testing build permits live reads, explicit watchlist star toggles, and user-reviewed lineup, supported conditional blind-bid, trade, board, supported FCFS add/drop and IR submissions. Each is sent directly to MFL and read back for confirmation. Offers and responses may notify other owners through MFL. A counteroffer is a new offer and leaves the original open; acceptance may require league approval or processing. MFL does not expose saved lineup tiebreakers. A multi-round waiver save can partially succeed; the app stops and asks you to compare saved versus drafted rounds. Unconfirmed posts, trades, watchlist changes and roster moves are not automatically resent. Unsupported waiver configurations and unverified trade details use the league website. The interactive Champion Hall preview uses local sample data and sends nothing to MFL.
